Recommended Methods

These are evalstats' current default recommendations, chosen automatically under method="auto" based on your data's estimand, data type, and sample size. They summarize the results of an extensive Monte Carlo simulation study, validated against real LLM eval data. Boxed methods are the default; gray notes give conservative alternatives. Both trees cover single-run, paired data at N=15–100.

95% Confidence Interval

What are you estimating a CI for?
  • Mean point estimate
    • Binary (0 / 1)
      Wilson score interval
    • Numeric
      Logit-t interval Conservative: NIG, smooth bootstrap
  • Mean of paired differences
    • Binary (0 / 1)
      Bonett–Price adjusted Wald Bonett & Price, 2012
    • Continuous
      Logit-t interval Conservative: smooth bootstrap
    • Likert / discrete
      Bayesian NIG

p-value / FWER Correction

How many comparisons are you running?
  • Single pairwise comparison
    • Binary (0 / 1)
      McNemar mid-p test Agrees with the CI beside it
    • Numeric
      Wilcoxon signed-rank test Conservative: sign test
  • Family of comparisons (k≥3)
    • Simultaneous CIs
      Šidák procedure Every n, every eval type
    • p-value correction
      • n < 30
        Shaffer's procedure
      • n ≥ 30
        Romano–Wolf step-down Romano & Wolf, 2005
